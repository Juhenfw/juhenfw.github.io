A Review: Artificial Intelligence Related to Agricultural Equipment Integrated with the Internet of Things

Published

Juhen Fashikha Wildan

Journal of Advanced Technology and Multidiscipline (JATM), Vol. 02, No. 02, 2023, pp. 47-60

E-ISSN: 2964-6162 | Publisher: Universitas Airlangga, Faculty of Advanced Technology and Multidiscipline

This comprehensive review paper analyzes the integration of AI technologies (Fuzzy Logic, ANN, ML, DL, GA, SVM, KNN, DSS) with IoT systems in agricultural equipment. The research covers applications in soil monitoring, irrigation, fertilizer spraying, pest control, harvesting, forecasting, and yield monitoring for the period 2019-2023.

Multi-microcontroller system for Mecanum robots with gripper-shooter mechanisms

Published

Juhen Fashikha Wildan

IAES International Journal of Robotics and Automation (IJRA), Vol. 14, Issue 2, pp. 181-190, 2025

E-ISSN: 2089-3272 | Publisher: IAES Institute of Advanced Engineering and Science

This research presents the design and implementation of a multi-microcontroller digital control system for a Mecanum-wheeled robot with gripper and shooter mechanisms, tailored for agricultural applications.
